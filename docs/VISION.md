This project is a single-player, browser-based adventure RPG centered on Jesus and the events of the Gospels, set in first-century Galilee and Judea and featuring an original low-poly 3D aesthetic inspired by classic Old School RuneScape. The visual direction will emphasize chunky silhouettes, broad areas of color, restrained textures, matte materials, simple lighting, and a warm, reverent atmosphere, with modular environments and characters created in Blender and exported as glTF/GLB assets. The game will be built with TypeScript, Babylon.js, and Vite, targeting WebGL 2 initially and running entirely on the client, with static hosting through GitHub Pages and no required backend, accounts, or multiplayer infrastructure. An elevated, rotatable camera based on Babylon’s ArcRotateCamera will accompany click-to-move controls, using A* pathfinding over an invisible walkability grid to navigate around buildings, water, and other obstacles without requiring a full physics engine. The architecture will separate gameplay simulation, 3D presentation, interface components, narrative content, and persistence into focused TypeScript modules; lightweight HTML/CSS overlays, optionally using React, will provide dialogue, inventory, a travel journal, settings, and menus without driving frame-by-frame scene updates. Quests and conversations will use authored, reviewable data and explicit state transitions, with Gospel references attached to relevant scenes and clear distinctions between direct scriptural quotations and original connective dialogue; the central narrative will not depend on runtime-generated dialogue. Local save slots will use IndexedDB through idb, with versioned save data, migrations, and save-file export/import to protect progress across updates or browser-storage loss. The world will be divided into regions loaded as needed, with reusable assets and early testing across target browsers and devices; offline play can later be added through a service worker that caches the application and downloaded regions. Development will begin with a small playable lakeside village containing character movement, camera controls, a few interactive characters, one complete quest, dialogue choices, a journal update, and save/load functionality, establishing the visual style and core gameplay before expanding into additional Gospel-based locations and episodes.
