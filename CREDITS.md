# Credits and provenance

The Galilee kit, procedural environment, interface icons, code, and connective narrative were created for this project. Models and animation are generated by `tools/blender/generate_kit.py` and `tools/blender/rigging.py`, with the episode workshop and exports produced through Blender MCP. No third-party models, textures, fonts, music, or recordings are included. The interface uses system fonts; optional ambience and chimes are synthesized in the browser.

The village is an artistic interpretation, not an archaeological reconstruction. Its original low-poly geometry uses no RuneScape assets or branding.

The narrated episode quotes Luke 5:1–11 from the public-domain [World English Bible](https://ebible.org/engwebp/LUK05.htm), preserving Jesus’ and Simon’s words within the account. The prelude also quotes Luke 5:4. Exact verses are identified in `src/content/episode/scripture.ts`; surrounding conversations and journal prose are original. References identify inspiration without making invented errands historical claims.
