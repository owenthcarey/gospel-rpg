# Credits and provenance

The Galilee kit, procedural environment, interface icons, code, and connective narrative were created for this project. Models and animation are generated by `tools/blender/generate_kit.py`, `rigging.py`, `neighborhood.py`, `life.py`, `road.py`, `galilee.py`, and `crossing.py` through Blender MCP. The Road to Nain assets and workshop were recreated and inspected through MCP after restoring its addon connection, replacing the initial CLI exports. No third-party models, textures, music, or recordings are included. The interface uses three bundled open-license typefaces (see [Fonts](#fonts)); its logo, ornaments and icons are original inline SVG. The original soundtrack, instrument timbres, regional ambience, and sound effects are synthesized in the browser. Nine new compositions in `src/content/audio/music.ts` were authored for this project, including their melodies, harmonies, and arrangements. They evoke the small synthetic instrument palette of early browser RPGs without using any existing game melodies, soundfonts, or recordings. See [Music and sound](docs/AUDIO.md) for the track list and reproducible rendering workflow.

The village is an artistic interpretation, not an archaeological reconstruction. Its original low-poly geometry uses no RuneScape assets or branding.

The narrated episode quotes Luke 5:1–11 from the public-domain [World English Bible](https://ebible.org/engwebp/LUK05.htm), preserving Jesus’ and Simon’s words within the account. Chapter II quotes Mark 2:1–12 from the public-domain [World English Bible](https://ebible.org/engwebp/MRK02.htm); its source text is in `src/content/campaign/scripture.ts`. Luke 5:17–26 is a separately attributed parallel. Chapter III quotes Luke 7:11–17 from the public-domain [World English Bible](https://ebible.org/engwebp/LUK07.htm), stored in `src/content/road/scripture.ts`. Chapter IV quotes all seven verses of Mark 4:35–41 from the public-domain [World English Bible](https://ebible.org/engwebp/MRK04.htm), stored in `src/content/lake/scripture.ts`. Joel, Dalia and the ordinary crossing are original connective fiction. The prelude’s optional reading quotes Luke 5:4. Exact Chapter I verses are identified in `src/content/episode/scripture.ts`; surrounding conversations and journal prose are original. References identify inspiration without making invented errands historical claims.

## Fonts

RFC-011 bundles three typefaces from pinned [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org) packages. Only the Latin subset and the weights listed are imported (`src/ui/fonts.ts`); Vite self-hosts the files in the build, so no font is requested from another origin at runtime.

| Typeface      | Package                           | Weights used              | License     | Copyright                                                   |
| ------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cinzel        | `@fontsource/cinzel` 5.3.0        | 500, 600                  | SIL OFL 1.1 | 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors (NDISCOVER)                 |
| Alegreya      | `@fontsource/alegreya` 5.3.0      | 400, 400 italic, 500, 700 | SIL OFL 1.1 | 2011 The Alegreya Project Authors (Huerta Tipográfica)      |
| Alegreya Sans | `@fontsource/alegreya-sans` 5.3.0 | 400, 500, 700             | SIL OFL 1.1 | 2013 The Alegreya Sans Project Authors (Huerta Tipográfica) |

The full license text ships with each package (`node_modules/@fontsource/*/LICENSE`) and is available at [openfontlicense.org](https://openfontlicense.org). The fonts are used unmodified.

Development tools retain the licenses supplied with their distributions. Exact JavaScript dependency versions are in `package-lock.json`.
