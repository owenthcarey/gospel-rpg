# Credits and provenance

The Galilee kit, procedural environment, interface icons, code, and connective narrative were created for this project. Models and animation are generated by `tools/blender/generate_kit.py`, `rigging.py`, `neighborhood.py`, `life.py`, and `road.py` through Blender MCP. The Road to Nain assets and workshop were recreated and inspected through MCP after restoring its addon connection, replacing the initial CLI exports. No third-party models, textures, fonts, music, or recordings are included. The interface uses system fonts; optional ambience and chimes are synthesized in the browser.

The village is an artistic interpretation, not an archaeological reconstruction. Its original low-poly geometry uses no RuneScape assets or branding.

The narrated episode quotes Luke 5:1–11 from the public-domain [World English Bible](https://ebible.org/engwebp/LUK05.htm), preserving Jesus’ and Simon’s words within the account. Chapter II quotes Mark 2:1–12 from the public-domain [World English Bible](https://ebible.org/engwebp/MRK02.htm); its source text is in `src/content/campaign/scripture.ts`. Luke 5:17–26 is a separately attributed parallel. Chapter III quotes Luke 7:11–17 from the public-domain [World English Bible](https://ebible.org/engwebp/LUK07.htm), stored in `src/content/road/scripture.ts`. The prelude’s optional reading quotes Luke 5:4. Exact Chapter I verses are identified in `src/content/episode/scripture.ts`; surrounding conversations and journal prose are original. References identify inspiration without making invented errands historical claims.
